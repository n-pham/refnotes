# Why Number 0 Was Invented

At first, ancient people such as the Mayas did not have numbers at all.

5 days is written:\
`☼ ☼ ☼ ☼ ☼`

Then they invented numbers to shorten the writing. With 5 as `-`, 5 days is written:\
`- x ☼` 

When the counts increased, the same numbers were stacked up in rows with the higher position being 20 times bigger, which could easily get mixed up:
```
    5 days (x ☼)    or    100 days (x ☼)
    -                       -    <-- 5 x 20 = 100
(end)                            <-- blank
                        (end)
```

The number 0 was invented to fill the empty spot to make the counts clear:

```
    5 days (x ☼)    or    100 days (x ☼)
    -                       -    <-- 5 x 20 = 100
(end)                       𝋠    <-- 𝋠 is number 0 instead of blank
                        (end)
```
